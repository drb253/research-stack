## Table and figure captions

Place captions where the venue expects them (immediately below the table, or on a separate figure
page) and reference each display in the text at least once. Caption template:

```markdown
Table 1. Characteristics of included studies. NSCLC, non-small-cell lung cancer; RCT, randomised
controlled trial; HR, hazard ratio; CI, confidence interval. Effect estimates are reported as
published; no pooling was performed.
```

## Drafting order that avoids rework

1. Methods (it depends only on what you actually did, not on the findings).
2. Body themes, from the strongest evidence to the weakest.
3. Discussion.
4. Conclusions.
5. Introduction and abstract last, so they describe the review that exists.
6. Displays after the ledgers are final.
7. Citations numbered by `build_reference_list.py`, then the gate.
